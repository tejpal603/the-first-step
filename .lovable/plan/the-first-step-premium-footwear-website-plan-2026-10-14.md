# The First Step — Premium Footwear Website Plan

## Direction

Create a complete, enquiry-first footwear website with a polished editorial fashion aesthetic: deep black and warm ivory foundations, restrained champagne-gold details, elegant serif display type, clean sans-serif supporting type, and premium footwear photography.

Because inventory, prices, policies, hours, reviews, and online ordering are not yet confirmed, the first version will clearly label editable catalogue content and owner-required details rather than presenting them as facts. Shopify setup was skipped, so this phase will not include a cart or checkout.

## Pages and customer journey

- Build a shared sticky header, mobile menu, WhatsApp/call shortcuts, and refined black footer.
- Create distinct pages for Home, Men, Women, Formal Shoes, Casual Shoes, Sneakers, and Contact, with working navigation between them.
- Home will include the requested editorial hero, category gallery, collection preview, reasons to shop, brand story, store location, Google rating summary, owner-completion FAQ, and final call to action.
- Category pages will use premium imagery and clearly marked sample catalogue cards without claiming stock, brands, prices, sizes, or offers.
- Contact will prioritize directions, click-to-call, WhatsApp, the verified address, a map, and a validated product-enquiry form that opens a prefilled WhatsApp conversation.
- Add dedicated informational pages for privacy, terms, delivery, and returns, each clearly marked where owner confirmation is required.

## Visual and interaction system

- Generate a cohesive set of realistic luxury footwear images for the main banner, category cards, product previews, brand story, and closing feature.
- Define all colors, typography, spacing, focus styles, and shadows as reusable design tokens.
- Use subtle reveal motion, image hover zoom, polished button transitions, and a smooth mobile menu while respecting reduced-motion settings.
- Ensure stable layouts and readable controls across desktop, tablet, and mobile, with keyboard-friendly menus, accordions, and forms.

## Trust and local conversion

- Use only the supplied address, phone number, rating, and review count, with a visible note that the rating should be reconfirmed before launch.
- Link directions to the supplied address and provide direct call and WhatsApp actions throughout the site.
- Do not fabricate review quotes; show the supplied aggregate rating and route visitors to the public Google review listing.
- Keep opening hours, social links, delivery coverage, accepted payments, and return/exchange terms visibly pending owner confirmation.

## Search visibility

- Give every page unique local-search-focused metadata, social metadata, semantic headings, and descriptive image text.
- Add verified LocalBusiness structured data without unsupported claims or unconfirmed hours.
- Provide a sitemap and retain an appropriate robots file.
- Use natural internal links among collection, store, and policy pages.

## Technical implementation

- Build with the existing TanStack Start and Tailwind v4 setup, using small reusable sections and data-driven placeholder catalogue content.
- Use the existing icon library for navigation and action controls.
- Keep enquiry interactions client-side and route submissions into WhatsApp; no customer data will be stored in this phase.
- Verify the central browsing and enquiry journey, responsive layouts, keyboard behavior, page metadata, and final preview after implementation.

## Owner details still needed before launch

- Confirmed weekly opening hours.
- Actual categories, brands, products, prices, sizes, colors, and availability.
- Delivery area, charges, payment methods, and return/exchange policy.
- Permitted authentic review excerpts and direct Google review URL.
- Verified Instagram and Facebook accounts.
- A Shopify store connection if online catalogue, inventory, cart, and checkout are required later.
